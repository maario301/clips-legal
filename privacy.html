Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 June 2026

N8N Mario Clips ("the App") is a self-hosted personal automation that helps a single content creator repurpose their own long-form videos into short vertical clips and publish them to their own TikTok profile and their own YouTube channel. This policy explains what data the App handles.

The App's use of information received from Google APIs (including YouTube API Services) adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. By using the App you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's own handling of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

1. Who we are

The App is operated privately by its owner for their own content. There is no commercial service, no sign-up, and no third-party users. The only person who authorizes and uses the App is its owner.

2. Data we access

  • TikTok account info (user.info.basic): open id, avatar and display name, used only to confirm which account has been connected.
  • Video content you provide: the source videos you upload are processed locally (transcription, clipping, subtitling) to generate short clips.
  • TikTok access and refresh tokens: stored locally to publish clips on your behalf via the TikTok Content Posting API.
  • YouTube (youtube.upload scope): an OAuth 2.0 access/refresh token, stored locally, used only to upload the generated clip to the owner's own YouTube channel via the videos.insert endpoint of the YouTube Data API v3. The App does not read, collect or store any other YouTube data, and never uploads to any channel other than the owner's.

3. How we use it

Data is used solely to generate clips and publish them to the accounts of the user who authorized the App: to TikTok via the video.upload and video.publish scopes, and to the owner's own YouTube channel via the youtube.upload scope. The App does not post on behalf of anyone else.

4. Storage and sharing

All processing happens on the owner's own machine/server. We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, and we do not transfer Google user data to anyone. Video transcription may use third-party AI services (e.g. speech-to-text and an LLM) strictly to process the content you provide.

5. Retention and deletion

Source videos, generated clips and tokens are kept only as long as needed and can be deleted at any time by the owner. You can revoke the App's access at any time from your TikTok account settings or from your Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions), which invalidates the stored tokens.
