N8N Mario Clips

A self-hosted personal automation that turns your own long-form videos into short vertical clips with subtitles and publishes them to your own TikTok profile.

How it works

  • You upload a source video through a private form.
  • The app transcribes it, picks the best 20-30s moments, and edits them into 9:16 clips with burned-in subtitles.
  • Using TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API (Direct Post), each clip is published to the profile of the user who authorized the app.

The app only ever posts to the profile of the user who connected it. It is a personal, non-commercial project.
